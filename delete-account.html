Account & data

Delete Your Account

Account deletion is not performed inside the My Memory Verse application. Version 2.1.0 does not delete Firebase or cloud data from the app. A control in Settings can open this page, but the request itself is sent by email and handled manually. Last updated November 15, 2026.

Request deletion by email

Email mymemoryverseapp@gmail.com and include the Google account email associated with My Memory Verse. Use the subject line Account Deletion Request - My Memory Verse.

Email a deletion request

How to request deletion

The app does not delete your account. If Settings offers Request Account Deletion, that control only opens this page. Send the request to the official support address. The developer manually verifies that the request relates to your account and then processes it.

  1. Open your email app.
  2. Send a message to mymemoryverseapp@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request - My Memory Verse.
  3. Include the Google account email associated with My Memory Verse. This is required so the account can be identified. Also include your community username if you created one.
  4. Send the email. The request is not completed when the message is sent.

If the button above does not open an email app, copy mymemoryverseapp@gmail.com and send the message yourself with that subject line.

What the developer does

Deletion is a manual process. After the Google account email is checked against the My Memory Verse account, the developer reviews the associated Firebase records and deletes the account data tied to that verified account. This includes the Firebase Authentication record and Cloud Firestore data associated with the account, such as the profile, cloud-synchronized favorites, custom verses saved to the account, memorization streaks, practice statistics, and leaderboard entries.

Requests are processed within a reasonable period after there is enough information to verify them. If the Google account email or other details are missing, the developer may reply and ask for them before deleting anything. Sending the email does not delete the account instantly.

Shared community verses

Shared community verses may require a separate administrative action. They are not removed automatically when the account record is deleted.

A shared verse may be anonymized rather than deleted, so the Scripture post can remain available to the community without your username and avatar. If you need a specific community verse reviewed, say so in the deletion email.

What this request does not do

  • Uninstalling the app does not delete cloud data. Cloud favorites, custom verses, streaks, practice statistics, and the account remain until a verified deletion request is processed.
  • Signing out does not delete your account or cloud data.
  • Information stored only on your device, including a local block list, is not removed by the cloud deletion. Clear the app’s storage on the device, or uninstall the app, to remove that local copy.
  • Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Premium Monthly is an auto-renewing subscription billed by Google Play. Cancel it separately in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Google explains cancellation at Google Play Help.
  • Google may retain Play Billing records under Google’s own policies. Firebase Analytics data that can no longer be tied to the deleted account may remain for the retention period configured in Firebase.

How long it takes

Requests are processed within a reasonable period. The time depends on verifying the Google account email and manually reviewing the Firebase records. You will not see the account disappear at the moment you send the email.

After processing, signing in again with the same Google account can create a new My Memory Verse account. It will not restore cloud data that has already been deleted. Anonymized community verses are not restored to your username.
