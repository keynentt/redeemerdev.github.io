Community Guidelines

The Community Feed is a Scripture-focused space for sharing Bible verses that help others read, remember, and practice God’s Word. Please keep it respectful and appropriate for a Christian community.

Keep the Community Respectful

By sharing in the Community Feed, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines and the Terms of Service.

What the Community Feed is for

Share Bible verses so others can discover Scripture, save Favorites, and practice memorization. Keep posts related to that purpose.

Please do not share or use the Community Feed for

  • Harassment or bullying
  • Threats
  • Hate or hateful content
  • Sexually explicit or pornographic content
  • Spam
  • Scams or fraud
  • Impersonation
  • Illegal content
  • Malicious content
  • Copyright infringement
  • Abusive behavior
  • Content that is inappropriate for the intended Christian Scripture-focused community

Usernames

Your Community username is permanent and public. Please keep it respectful and appropriate for a Christian community.

Reporting and moderation

Signed-in users may report Community Feed posts or inappropriate display names in the app. Reports help us review potential problems.

My Memory Verse / Redeemer Dev may remove content that violates these Guidelines or the Terms of Service, and may restrict or suspend accounts when appropriate. Submitting a report does not guarantee that content will be removed.

Related pages

These Guidelines work together with the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Questions can be sent to mymemoryverseapp@gmail.com.
